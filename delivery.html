Delivery FAQs

Common questions about our delivery service, answered.

How fast can I get my groceries delivered?
Standard delivery takes 90 minutes to 2 hours depending on your zone. Express delivery guarantees your order within 2 hours, and city-center orders often arrive in as little as 30 minutes.
Is delivery really free on orders over $50?
Yes! Orders over $50 ship free in City Center and Suburbs. Outer Ring and Extended Areas have a small flat fee of $4.99–$6.99 to cover longer routes.
Can I choose a specific delivery time?
Absolutely. At checkout, choose from 2-hour windows for today, tonight, or schedule up to 14 days ahead. Plus members get priority access to popular slots.
What if I'm not home when the driver arrives?
Our drivers will call or message you. If you prefer, choose contactless delivery at checkout and we'll leave your order in a safe, insulated spot you select.
Are fresh items packed safely?
Yes. All chilled and frozen items travel in insulated bags with ice packs. Fresh produce is hand-packed in breathable crates to keep everything crisp.
Can I cancel or change my order?
You can modify or cancel your order up to 1 hour before your delivery slot from your dashboard — no questions asked.
